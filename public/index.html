How a turn works
  1. Set your eight. From your 12 cards pick 5 starters (2 frontcourt PF/C, 3 backcourt PG/SG/SF) and a 3-player bench (1 frontcourt, 2 backcourt). The other four sit out. Your opponent sees only card backs.
  2. Subs: only after a successful attack (that turn) or a successful defense (your next turn), frontcourt for frontcourt and backcourt for backcourt. Resting players get 1 stamina bar back each turn.
  3. 0 defense: a starter whose inside or outside defense hits 0 must be subbed out. On the bench that defense recovers 20% per turn, and they can't come back while it is still 0.
  4. Attack once. Pick a starter, choose inside or outside, then pick a target. Both cards flip.
  5. Stamina: every starter has 5 bars. Each attack burns one and cuts all their ratings by 10%. No bars left, no more attacks.
  6. Score: attack beats defense, the difference hits their HP and comes off the defender's rating for that shot type.
  7. Wide open: if no healthy bench player can replace them, a defender at 0 lets the full attack through to HP.
  8. Stopped: defense wins, the difference is taken off your scorer's rating for that shot type.
  9. Every name is unique: you and your opponent never share a player.
