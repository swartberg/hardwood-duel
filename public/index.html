How a turn works
  1. Draft. You have 20 credits. Packs cost 5 (Superstars), 4 (All-Stars), 3 (Starters), 2 (Role Players) or 1 (Bench Warmers). Each pack shows 5 players of that tier and you keep 1. Draft 8 players: 3 frontcourt (PF, C) and 5 backcourt (PG, SG, SF).
  2. Set your lineup. 5 starters (2 frontcourt, 3 backcourt) and a 3-player bench (1 frontcourt, 2 backcourt). Your opponent sees only card backs.
  3. Subs: only after a successful attack (that turn) or a successful defense (your next turn), frontcourt for frontcourt and backcourt for backcourt. Resting players get 1 stamina bar back each turn.
  4. Attack once. Pick a starter, choose inside or outside, then pick a target. Both cards flip face up for the rest of that turn, then turn face down again.
  5. Stamina: every starter has 5 bars. Each attack burns one and cuts all their ratings by 10%. No bars left, no more attacks.
  6. Out of position: a backcourt player attacking the frontcourt (or the reverse) leaves their own group exposed. During the opponent's next turn, everyone in that group defends at −30%.
  7. Score: attack beats defense, the difference hits their HP and comes off the defender's rating for that shot type. Defense never drops below 20.
  8. Stopped: defense wins, the difference is taken off your scorer's rating for that shot type.
